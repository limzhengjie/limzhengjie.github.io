# Experience logos

Local copies of company logos identify the employers in the homepage Experience section. Company names remain in image alt text and hover titles. No third-party logo requests are made by visitors.

Sources retrieved 30 October 2026:

- `artemis.svg`: inline navigation wordmark from https://about.artemis.ai/ (the official Artemis Analytics website redirects here). The site's existing monochrome filter supplies its dark-theme treatment.
- `paymongo.svg` and `paymongo-dark.svg`: https://www.paymongo.com/company/press/brand/paymongo-logo.svg and https://www.paymongo.com/company/press/brand/paymongo-logo-reversed.svg.
- `fifth-person.png` and `fifth-person-dark.png`: https://fifthperson.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/The-Fifth-Person.png and https://fifthperson.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/fifthperson-logo-white.png.
- `uob.svg`: https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/iwov-resources/assets/BrandAssets/UOB_Logo.svg.
- `sas.svg`: inline navigation logo from https://www.sas.com/en_us/home.html.

The original artwork is retained. PayMongo and Fifth Person use their supplied light/dark variants; the logo display follows both system theme preferences and the site's saved theme override.
