Revenue per employee across seven companies

Zheng Jie Lim · Artemis · Data as of Apr 2026

This infographic places revenue, employee counts and revenue per employee side by side for seven companies: Hyperliquid, Valve, OnlyFans, XTX Markets, Tether, Jane Street and Anthropic.

The horizontal bars make the differences in reported scale easy to compare. Revenue per employee is one lens on a business: its interpretation depends on the revenue definition, reporting period and headcount used.

Sources and context

The original graphic is 2025-focused and dated April 2026. Its footnotes identify OnlyFans’ FY2024 figures and Anthropic’s December 2025 annualized revenue run-rate. Reporting periods and revenue definitions differ across the comparison, and some headcounts are estimates.

Source credits in the original: Artemis, Business Standard, Tracxn, Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance and Fortune, as credited in the original graphic.
