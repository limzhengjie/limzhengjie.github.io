Where x402 payments settle

Zheng Jie Lim · Artemis · Data as of 5 May 2026

Which chains and stablecoins carry x402 payments? This infographic presents two views of the settlement mix: Base compared with other chains, and USDC compared with other stablecoins.

The charts sit alongside cumulative payment counts, payment volume and merchant figures in the original artwork. Together, they show the concentration of settlement infrastructure in the source’s x402 dataset.

Sources and context

The snapshot is dated 5 May 2026, with cumulative totals described as starting in October 2025. Its figures refer to the source’s observed x402 activity; they do not measure all AI-agent commerce or all stablecoin payments.
