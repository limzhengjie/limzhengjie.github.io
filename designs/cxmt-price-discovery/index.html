CXMT pre-IPO price discovery on Hyperliquid

Zheng Jie Lim · Artemis · Data as of 27 Jul 2026

This historical case study compares the CXMT pre-IPO perpetual market on Hyperliquid with the opening price in Shanghai. It looks at how a continuously traded derivatives market priced an event in a traditional equity market.

The main chart follows implied valuation before the opening bell. An inset focuses on the final hour, highlighting the observation one minute before the Shanghai market opened.
